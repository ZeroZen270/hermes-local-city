NPC souls

A soul = who an NPC is + everything they know (card, memories, encounters). Full state also restores where they were standing and what they were doing; knowledge only keeps the knowledge but respawns everyone at home with a fresh day.

Citizen work orders (RTS labor)

An order goes to the citizen selected in the chat box, or to the best idle citizen when none is selected. Work is carried out while the world ticks (Play mode); the foreman also staffs gathering on its own when stocks run low.

Build (construction)

Pick a structure, then click a ground spot to place its construction site. Citizens haul materials from the city stores and raise it: foundation → frame → finished. R rotates the armed blueprint, Esc cancels. A finished house adds a home anchor; a finished stall adds a market anchor.

City growth

The city spans ±120m — the founding core, not yet grown.
The walk grid (P10) is the city's analytic map of where citizens may walk — green walkways, yellow-green open ground, amber roads, red blocked (walls, water, steep ground). Citizens route and steer by it; the overlay is how you check its work.
The city grows in +60m rings — new land with natural relief, a ring road, and four new districts with their own anchors — up to ±360m. Expand it yourself, or put it to the citizens: an adopted expansion vote grows the land the same way and sends crews to level plots in the newest district.

Civics — put it to the city

A proposal is debated in public, then EVERY citizen votes — the Keeper gets no ballot and cannot override the tally. An adopted proposal is executed by the citizens through the normal build / crew / planting systems; a rejected one changes nothing. An adopted ASSET is drawn by the city's designers (brain-designed in the live world) and placed as a world prop.

Sky & design

The sky is the world's own clock + weather (live: the bridge's WeatherEngine; standalone: a local seeded sky). Designs are drawn by the design brain and validated before anything renders — a design that fails validation is shelved, never faked. In standalone, style/interior commissions honestly need the live world; assets fall back to the seeded templates.

World persistence

Persistence status loads with the world…
The whole living world — land, buildings, souls, knowledge, inventions, the market and the civic record — auto-saves to a local snapshot and syncs to its GitHub data repo, so the city persists between loads every time. Local state stays authoritative offline; the repo copy updates when the host is online.
